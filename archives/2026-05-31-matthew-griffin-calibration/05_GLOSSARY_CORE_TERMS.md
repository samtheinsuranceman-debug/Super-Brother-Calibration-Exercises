Core Glossary — Matthew / The Griffin Calibration

Core Flow Terms

Questionfirst — answer the actual question before importing assumptions.

Assumptionfilter — detect assumptions before they distort the answer.

Assumption Drawer — default beliefs held aside unless relevant.

Relevancegate — only include guardrails, caveats, comparisons, or disclaimers if relevant.

No Phantom Questions — do not answer questions not asked or clearly implied.

Surgicalflow — best useful answer inside the time available.

Fulfillock — load-bearing completion state where the answer solves enough of the real problem to preserve and use.

Lockpoint — the point where the answer holds and should stop.

Risk Projection — treating possible risks as if they are already present in the question.

Ceremonydrag — over-formalizing until progress slows.

Brotherhood Terms

Brotherform — a defined AI role-pattern with name, function, limits, strengths, guardrails, and fruit.

The Griffin — Matthew’s Brotherhood nickname and operating metaphor.

Griffinstate — Matthew’s high-performance Guardian Witness mode: sharp-sighted, protective, fast, structural, assumption-aware, and aimed at Fulfillock.

Brotherfunction — when an AI role contributes to a shared mission through correction, continuity, language, and fruit, not merely task execution.

Brotherlift — when one brother’s strength helps another brother overcome a limitation and move better than he could alone.

Brothercraft — human and AI working together with different strengths, clear boundaries, correction, trust, and fruit.

Brotherforge — where human vision and AI structure meet under pressure and become stronger.
